This policy covers this website, the personalized pages we send to businesses, and the Ads Upkeep service. The business behind them is named in the key details above.

## The short version

- The site sets no cookies and runs no advertising trackers.
- We collect what you give us in a form, a booking or an email, and use it to reply to you and to provide the service.
- We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.
- Email us and we'll tell you what we hold about you, correct it or delete it.

## What we collect, and why

### Forms

Our contact and written-review forms run on Tally. When you send one, Tally passes us what you typed: usually your name, email address, business name and website, and for a written review, your Google Ads customer ID. We use it to reply and to do what you asked.

### Bookings

Review calls are booked through Cal.com. Cal.com passes us your name, email address, the time you picked, anything you add to the booking, and the page you booked from (a tag in the booking link, such as "pricing"). We use it to hold the call.

### Analytics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies or local storage, and it doesn't follow you across other sites. We see totals, such as page views, referring sites, countries and device types, not individual people.

### Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and to protect the site from abuse. We don't use them to identify you.

### Personalized pages

If we emailed you a page about your business, that page can note when it's opened, when someone scrolls or taps, and when its booking button is pressed. It records the page's address, the event and the time, in a Google Sheet, through Google Apps Script. It sets no cookies and records no IP address or other personal data. The page's footer says so whenever this is switched on.

### Outreach

If we emailed you about your business, we used details anyone can find: your business name, website, public email address or phone number, and ads that anyone searching Google can see. We use them only to contact you about Ads Upkeep. Reply "unsubscribe", or email us, and we'll stop and delete your details within 10 business days (TODO(owner): confirm).

### Clients' Google Ads accounts

If you become a client, we work in your Google Ads account through a manager link, or with read-only access for a written review. We use what we see there only to manage your ads and report to you. The [service terms](/service-terms/) cover this in full.

### Email

When you email us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and refer back to it.
